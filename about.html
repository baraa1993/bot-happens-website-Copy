Our point of view

Good support is designed.

Bot Happens grew out of more than 6 years on the front lines of customer support. After resolving thousands of tickets, recurring friction in routing, workflows, documentation, and reporting became impossible to ignore. The work now focuses on improving those systems—not just working around them.

The practice brings together support process design, platform configuration, automation thinking, documentation, and performance visibility, informed by hands-on experience with Intercom, Zendesk, HubSpot, and Salesforce. The goal is practical: help your people do their best work and make every customer interaction easier to manage.

We believe technology should reduce friction, not create more of it. The right solution is the one your team can understand, use, and sustain.
